03 / WHAT I AM MEASURING

Structure is not
the same as support.

I built EvidenceShift to make the difference inspectable: what the model received, what it returned, and whether its answer follows from the cited source.

Read the full methodology ↗
01

Hold the evidence fixed

Keep the question, passage text, source IDs, model, and request options fixed. Reverse only the passage order.

02

Separate the checks

Inspect completion and citation membership separately from factual support, conditions, relevance, and completeness.

03

Retain the original output

Keep failed completions, false abstentions, and wrong answers. Review against saved passages without replacing failures with favorable reruns.

What this explorer establishes

It presents recorded outcomes from limited local experiments. The fixed-context comparisons do not test new document ingestion or retrieval. Single outputs per order are not a population-level causal estimate. The reserved questions were not independently held out.
